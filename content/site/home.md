I'm a student who likes to learn, build, and explore things simply because I'm
curious about them.

Academically, I'm drawn to technology, programming, and machine learning. I
enjoy understanding how things work beneath the surface, experimenting with
ideas, and turning what I learn into projects that I can actually build and
improve. I care about having strong fundamentals, but I also believe some of the
best learning happens when you stop following instructions and start figuring
things out yourself.

Outside academics, I'm interested in music, creativity, culture, and the little
things that make life interesting. Music, in particular, is a big part of how I
unwind and experience the world — I'm as likely to spend time discovering an
artist or a song as I am to spend an evening working on a project.

I'm still figuring out exactly where I want this journey to take me, and I think
that's part of the fun. For now, I'm focused on learning deeply, building things
I'm proud of, staying curious, and becoming someone who can take an idea from
*"what if?"* to something real.
