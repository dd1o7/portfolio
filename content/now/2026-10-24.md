**What I learned that wasn't in the plan**

The ThermalLens result I'm most pleased with isn't the accuracy figure. It's
that the network and the classical fit make the *same* errors on the same data,
correlated at 0.975 — which means the limit is the measurements rather than the
method. I went looking for a performance number and found a statement about the
data instead.
